Justin Ottesen

Welcome to my personal website, where I document my exploration of software domains. My motivation for doing this is to broaden my knowledge. Most of my learning recently has been driven by my job, where I tend to dive deep with a narrow focus. This project will give me a new, wider area with room to roam, while providing a place to demonstrate concrete results and progress. My first focus will be web development, which conveniently makes this site both the record and the first project.

I am not starting from scratch here, I have both a bachelor's and a master's degree in computer science from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, which I pursued after I discovered my interest in software by teaching myself Python and JavaScript through middle and high school. I am a software engineer at Nasuni, working on a global versioned filesystem, accessed through network-attached storage appliances that cache data locally for performance. My work there has mostly focused on the data propagation algorithms that ensure various levels of consistency across sites. I have also worked on data retention, both the snapshot policies customers configure and the safe, efficient deletion of expired versions, and on garbage collection of cloud objects orphaned by failed or interrupted pushes.

All that to say, I have experience working on large-scale enterprise software, but that is all deep in the systems programming and design domain, so the web is a new, but similar area for me to dive into.

There isn't much here yet, but I hope to build this out to be a nice portfolio of my projects and learning. I don't plan to assume much prior knowledge, but often I will give very surface level descriptions and link out to other sources for more information, which I think will be more useful for readers than if I tried to explain everything myself.
